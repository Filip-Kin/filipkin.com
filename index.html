Robotics

I've been volunteering with FIRST for 7 years, and in that time I've attended 78 events. I've made some things to help my events run smoother, and my job easier.

FTA Buddy

ftabuddy.comGitHub

The field monitor that event staff keep open on their phones. It shows how long each robot has been disconnected, pulls match logs to share with a team, keeps inspection and radio checklists in sync across devices, and walks a volunteer through troubleshooting trees for the radio, roboRIO, CAN and power. A support chat answers from the WPILib, REV and CTRE docs and from past support threads.

Svelte, Bun, tRPC, Postgres

frc.toolsGitHub

One place for the things FRC teams keep searching for: a directory of community tools, photo albums grouped by event, a map of practice fields, off-season events, grants teams can apply for, and an archive of robot code and CAD.

FRC Radio Kiosk

A power board for the station that programs team radios at events. Version 1 is built. Version 2 is a routed 4-layer board for the new radio: it detects 802.3at PoE or falls back to passive 12 V, and a CR123A backup rides through a firmware update if the USB-C supply drops.

KiCad, STM32
